##### **4. Geometry** 📐
```javascript
Examples:
```

##### **5. Order of Operations (PEMDAS)** 🎯
```javascript
Examples:
- 3 + 2 × 4 = ? → 11 (not 20!)

- 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = ? → 8
- 5 + 3² = ? → 14
- (10 - 2) ÷ 4 + 1 = ? → 3

Progressive complexity:
Level 1: Two operations (3 + 2 × 4)
Level 2: Three operations with parentheses
Level 3: Exponents included
Level 4: Nested parentheses
Level 5: Complex expressions
```

##### **6. Exponents & Roots** 🚀
```javascript
Examples:
- 2³ = ? → 8
- √16 = ? → 4

- ∛27 = ? → 3
- 5⁰ = ? → 1

Laws of exponents:
- x² × x³ = x⁵
- (x²)³ = x⁶
- x⁶ ÷ x² = x⁴
- x⁻² = 1/x²
```

##### **8. Mental Math Tricks** 🧠
```javascript
Tricks to teach:

Multiplying by 11:
- 23 × 11 = 253 (split digits, add them in middle)
- 45 × 11 = 495

Squaring numbers ending in 5:
- 25² = 625 (2×3=6, append 25)
- 35² = 1225 (3×4=12, append 25)
- 45² = 2025 (4×5=20, append 25)

Multiplying by 9:
- Use fingers! 9×3: Put down 3rd finger, see 2 and 7 = 27

Doubling and halving:
- 16 × 25 = 8 × 50 = 4 × 100 = 400

Adding consecutive numbers:
- 1+2+3+...+10 = 10×11÷2 = 55
```

##### **9. Sentence Structure** 🏗️
```
Types:

Simple:
- One subject + one verb
- "The dog barks."
- "She runs fast."

Compound:
- Two independent clauses + conjunction
- "I like pizza, and she likes pasta."
- "He studied hard, but he didn't pass."

Complex:
- Independent + dependent clause
- "Although it was raining, we went outside."
- "She smiled when she saw the gift."

Compound-Complex:
- Multiple independent + dependent
- "While I was sleeping, the phone rang, and mom answered it."

Activities:
- Identify sentence types
- Combine simple sentences
- Add dependent clauses
- Fix run-ons
- Vary sentence structure in paragraphs
```
